Field method
Plane: press the phone's back flat against the fracture surface, wait for the stability lock, record. The screen-normal is taken as the pole; the app resolves it to the lower hemisphere.

Lineation: lay the phone on the surface with the top edge pointing down-plunge along the striae or intersection, then record.

Keep clear of steel hammers, magnets and vehicles — the compass is magnetic. Recheck the sensor against a known bearing at each new outcrop.
Counting
Lower-hemisphere equal-area (Schmidt) projection by default; switch to Wulff for equal-angle work. Density is contoured in multiples of a uniform distribution, so a value of 6 means six times the poles you would expect by chance in that cone.
